APP NAME
FeelCamera - Film Camera

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Choose WARM or DUSK, preview the color, and capture your everyday moments.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Choose a film-inspired look before you shoot.

FeelCamera applies WARM and DUSK color to your live camera preview and captured photos. Pick a look, frame your shot, and save the moment to your device.

TWO FILM-INSPIRED LOOKS
Switch between WARM and DUSK while looking at the camera preview.

SIMPLE CAMERA CONTROLS
Take photos with the on-screen shutter or your phone's volume buttons. Adjust the zoom and use the flash when your device supports it.

MAKE IT YOUR CAMERA
Choose your starting film, turn the composition grid on or off, and adjust the shutter sound setting. Shutter sound availability depends on your device and regional requirements.

PHOTOS SAVED TO YOUR DEVICE
Photos are saved as JPEG files in your device's Pictures/FeelCamera folder. Tap the latest-photo thumbnail to open the photo in a compatible gallery app. Photo contents are not uploaded to Firebase or a developer server.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
The app has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no photo-count limit.

YOUR MONITORING CHOICES
Usage statistics and crash reporting are optional. You can choose them separately and change your choices in Settings. The camera works without either choice.

FeelCamera currently supports the rear camera on Android 10 and later. Camera access is required to take photos.
