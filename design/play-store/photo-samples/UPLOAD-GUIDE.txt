PHOTOGRAPHIC ACCURACY
The layouts preserve the whole photo and its original aspect ratio. Only proportional downsampling is applied for display. No exposure, contrast, saturation, white-balance, subject, or perspective edits are applied.
The two attachments are separate captures, so they are not labeled as a controlled before-and-after comparison or the identical shot.

REMAINING SCREENSHOTS

SOURCE AND EXPORT AUDIT
manifest.json records the source labels, dimensions, SHA-256 values, and exported assets.
build-assets.cjs contains the editable SVG layouts and export logic. It needs Node.js and sharp; a bundled module path can be supplied through FEELCAMERA_SHARP_MODULE.
Existing AI-generated feature artwork and its earlier ZIP package remain available as a separate alternative.
